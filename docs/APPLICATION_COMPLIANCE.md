# Campus Core — Application Compliance Requirements

**Version:** 0.1 | **Status:** Draft engineering/product policy, not legal certification | **Owner:** Samuel with Adimchi

## 1. Purpose and scope
Define the safeguards Campus Core must meet before a public pilot. This checklist is not legal advice and does not establish compliance with Nigerian law or any platform policy. Relevant laws, regulator guidance, institutional permissions, and third-party terms require current verification before launch.

## 2. Affiliation and accuracy
- State clearly that Campus Core is an independent student project unless UNIZIK formally authorizes an affiliation.
- Do not use university logos, seals, or 'official university portal' claims without permission.
- Distinguish **verified publisher identity**, **source-attributed content**, and **official university communication**; these are not equivalent.
- Show publisher/source, published and updated dates, department/level, and a correction/report route.
- Use fictional or authorized demo content; never invent real examination changes as facts.
- November 3–6 P0 uses fictional, clearly labeled demo data until permission is confirmed. Resource links must lead to reviewed public learning material; fictional attribution must never appear to be a real university endorsement. No data collection, submission form, or simulated publisher functionality is implied by the demo.

## 3. Content governance
- Require a named accountable publisher for public items.
- Document permissions to republish third-party announcements, course materials, and media.
- Restrict unpublished, withdrawn, or access-limited material.
- Define corrections, expiry, archive, takedown, and dispute workflows.
- Avoid unmoderated student uploads in the initial release.

## 4. Privacy and data protection
- Collect only information needed for the selected feature; public browsing should not require an account.
- Publish a privacy notice before collecting personal information or enabling analytics/cookies requiring disclosure or consent.
- Define data controller/contact, purposes, processors, retention, rights handling, and breach response as applicable.
- Assess Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable NDPC guidance with appropriate review before a real launch; do not assume all obligations are identical for every implementation.
- Protect publisher credentials and avoid exposing personal data through logs, search engines, or third-party integrations.

## 5. Security and accessibility
Follow `SECURITY_REQUIREMENTS.md`. Provide usable keyboard navigation, readable text, accessible forms, and adequate contrast; use WCAG 2.2 AA as a design target, not an unverified certification.

## 6. Third-party services
Review terms, data processing, pricing, security, and allowed use before activating Supabase, Vercel, Google services, analytics, or AdSense. No ads or tracking should be added silently. AI features require a separate data-use and answer-reliability assessment.

## 7. Required evidence before release
- Approved affiliation wording and content-source policy.
- Permission records for real content.
- Privacy notice and contact channel if personal data is collected.
- Authorization and publication-state test evidence.
- Accessibility review and unresolved issues.
- Third-party services register and approved configuration.
- Completed `COMPLIANCE_AUDIT.md` and factual `COMPLIANCE_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md`.

## 8. Open decisions
Actual publisher approval authority; permitted content categories; privacy contact; retention period; correction SLA; institution authorization; external legal review; user analytics policy.
